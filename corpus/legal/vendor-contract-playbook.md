# Vendor Contract Playbook

## Liability caps

Standard position: vendor liability capped at 12 months of fees. Acceptable fallback: 6 months of fees. Escalate anything below 6 months to the General Counsel.

## Data processing

Any vendor touching customer data must sign the Halcyon DPA and provide a SOC 2 Type II report or equivalent.

## Payment terms

Standard payment terms are net 45. Net 30 is acceptable for strategic vendors.
