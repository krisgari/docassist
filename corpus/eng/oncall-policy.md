# Engineering On-call Policy

## Rotation

On-call shifts are one week long, starting Tuesday at 10:00 Pacific. Each team keeps a primary and a secondary on-call.

## Compensation

Engineers receive a stipend of 500 USD per on-call week, plus one day of time off for any week with more than three after-hours pages.

## Handoff

The outgoing on-call writes a handoff note in the team channel covering open incidents, flaky alerts and pending rollbacks.
