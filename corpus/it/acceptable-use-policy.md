# IT Acceptable Use Policy

## Devices

Company laptops must have full-disk encryption and automatic screen lock after 5 minutes. Personal devices may access email and Slack only through the managed mobile app.

## Passwords and MFA

Passwords must be at least 14 characters. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all company systems; hardware security keys are required for admins.

## AI tools

Only approved AI assistants may be used with company data. Do not paste confidential documents into unapproved tools.

## Reporting incidents

Report lost devices or suspected phishing to security@halcyon.example within 1 hour.
