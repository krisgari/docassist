# Parental Leave Policy (2026)

This policy replaces the 2023 parental leave FAQ and applies to all full-time employees from March 1, 2026.

## Eligibility

All full-time employees are eligible from their first day of employment. There is no minimum tenure requirement. Contractors and temporary staff are covered by their agency's policy, not this one.

## Leave entitlement

Primary caregivers receive 20 weeks of fully paid leave. Secondary caregivers receive 10 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave can be taken in up to three blocks within 12 months of the birth, adoption or foster placement.

| Caregiver type | Paid weeks | Blocks allowed | Must be used within |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | 20 | up to 3 | 12 months |
| Secondary | 10 | up to 3 | 12 months |

## Returning to work

Employees may return on a phased schedule of 60% hours at full pay for the first four weeks. Managers must approve phased return plans within five business days of the request.

## How to apply

Submit the leave request in Workday at least 30 days before the expected start date, or as soon as practical for adoption and foster placements. Questions go to the HR Business Partner for your organization.
