# Performance Review Process

## Cycle

Halcyon runs two review cycles per year: a mid-year check-in in July and a full annual review in January. Only the annual review affects compensation.

## Steps

1. Self-review in Lattice, due the second Friday of the cycle.
2. Peer feedback from three to five colleagues chosen with your manager.
3. Manager review and calibration with the department head.
4. Results conversation within two weeks of calibration closing.

## Ratings

The rating scale has four levels: Developing, Solid, Strong and Exceptional. Promotions are decided in the January cycle only.
