# Compensation Bands FY2026

Confidential. Shared with HR and the executive team only.

## Engineering bands

| Level | Title | Base salary range (USD) | Equity target (USD/yr) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E3 | Software Engineer | 135,000 – 165,000 | 20,000 |
| E4 | Senior Software Engineer | 170,000 – 210,000 | 45,000 |
| E5 | Staff Engineer | 215,000 – 260,000 | 90,000 |
| E6 | Principal Engineer | 265,000 – 320,000 | 160,000 |

## Sales bands

Account Executives (S3) have a base salary range of 95,000 to 120,000 USD with on-target earnings of 190,000 to 240,000 USD at a 50/50 base-to-variable split.

## Merit cycle

The FY2026 merit budget is 4.5% of base payroll. Increases take effect April 1, 2026.
