# Parental Leave FAQ (2023)

## How much leave do I get?

Primary caregivers receive 12 weeks of paid leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of paid leave.

## Do I need to have worked here for a while?

Yes. Employees must complete 6 months of service before becoming eligible for paid parental leave.

## Can I split my leave?

Leave must be taken in a single continuous block.
