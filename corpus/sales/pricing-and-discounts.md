# Pricing and Discount Approval Matrix

## List prices

The Fleet Platform costs 1,200 USD per robot per year. The Analytics add-on costs 300 USD per robot per year.

## Discount approvals

| Discount | Approver | Turnaround |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Up to 10% | Account Executive | Immediate |
| 10% to 20% | Regional Sales Director | 1 business day |
| 20% to 30% | VP Sales | 2 business days |
| Above 30% | COO | 5 business days, requires a deal review |

## Multi-year deals

Three-year commitments may receive an additional 5% discount without escalation, provided payment is annual in advance.
