# Customer Security FAQ

## Certifications

Halcyon holds a SOC 2 Type II report, renewed annually each October, and is ISO 27001 certified.

## Data residency

Customer telemetry is stored in the customer's chosen region: US (Oregon), EU (Frankfurt) or APAC (Sydney).

## Encryption

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest with AES-256. Customer-managed keys are available on the Enterprise plan.

## Data retention

Robot telemetry is retained for 13 months by default. Enterprise customers can set retention between 30 days and 7 years.
