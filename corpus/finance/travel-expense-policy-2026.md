# Travel and Expense Policy (2026)

## Flights

Economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class requires COO approval.

## Hotels

Nightly hotel caps are 275 USD in high-cost cities (San Francisco, New York, London, Tokyo) and 200 USD elsewhere.

## Meals

The daily meal allowance is 85 USD when traveling. Alcohol is not reimbursable except at approved client dinners.

## Submitting expenses

Submit expenses in Expensify within 30 days of the trip. Receipts are required for any item above 25 USD.
